04 · Three product paths

What you could offer
your audience.

Crossing what your content demonstrates with what your comments show people want.

Option 1 · The big bet

The Modern Poise & Presence Mentorship

What it is

An 8-week private group mentorship: live video feedback, posture evaluations and direct guidance on movement, grace and executive presence in high-stakes settings.

Who it's for

Women who want to command respect and project quiet confidence, and who want their own habits observed and corrected rather than described.

Why it sells

People can see what looks unrefined in others. Correcting it in themselves needs a trained eye watching them. That is a $1,500 problem, not a $50 one.

Option 2

The Social Diplomacy & Gathering Intensive

What it is

A focused workshop on conversational dynamics, hosting and social navigation: warmth, presence and inclusion without ever feeling out of place.

Who it's for

Women who host, network or attend events where the room is unfamiliar and the stakes are real.

Why it sells

Verbal and group dynamics rather than body mechanics. A natural second offer once the mentorship has a first cohort.

Option 3 · The $200 course

The Graceful Movement Course

What it is

A practical self-paced video course on daily movement: sitting, standing, walking and entering rooms with composure. Pairs with the $39 written guide as the entry step.

Who it's for

Followers who want the mechanics without the price or the commitment of live feedback.

Why it sells

Recorded once, sold repeatedly. The entry point that feeds the mentorship: every course buyer is a warm cohort applicant.

05 · The launch structure

How we'd fill
the twenty spots.

Six phases. Each one builds the belief needed for the next. The close at the end must be real; reopening destroys credibility for the next cohort.

  1. 1

    Build up

    Engage the community and signal that something is coming. Interactive stories, polls and Q&A boxes on the moments they feel awkward or unseen, alongside your own results.

  2. 2

    Invitation

    Announce it and invite the audience into a private WhatsApp or Telegram group where the details will be shared first. Mention a time-limited bonus for that group.

  3. 3

    Anticipation

    Build curiosity inside the group. Share the transformation people can expect and make the timeline crystal clear so everyone is ready the moment applications open.

  4. 4

    Reveal

    The full breakdown: what's included, the bonus, the price, the number of spots and the open/close dates. Leave no doubt about what's on the table.

  5. 5

    Conversion

    Share the application link for one of the twenty spots, run a poll to see who's in or has questions, and follow up one-to-one in the DMs with everyone who raises a hand. This is where most spots are filled.

  6. 6

    Urgency

    Countdown messages at 24h, 12h, 6h, 2h and closed. Make them picture the next important room they walk into without this. Then close for real.

Why an application, not a checkout

At $1,500 the buyer needs to feel chosen, not sold to. An application gives you a private conversation with every interested woman before she pays, which is where trust is built and where the cohort gets filled.

06 · Validate demand this week

Post this before
building anything.

One carousel. Black background, white text. If the replies come, we open applications. If they don't, you've spent ten minutes and we've learned something.

Slide 1 · The hook

You dress with care.

Yet when you walk into an important room, you still wonder if your posture gives away your doubt.

Slide 2 · The agitation

True elegance is never about buying louder clothes.

It is revealed in the quiet details: how you sit, how you bend, and how you hold space when all eyes turn to you.

Awkwardness happens when your habits betray your intentions.

Slide 3 · The ask

What if you had an exact framework to refine your posture, movement and presence with effortless calm?

Reply 'I WANT' if this resonates with you.

What the replies tell us

Every 'I WANT' is a named woman who has raised her hand before a single session exists. Twenty replies means the cohort fills. Ten means we run ten spots at a higher price or adjust the offer. Zero means we learned that cheaply, and we test a different angle next week. Those names are also the first people invited into the launch group.

Ready when you are.
